Who ate the most tokens?

Gotchis that join post their level and, per day, the food and XP they earned. Three lists, fair across Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, OpenCode and the other harnesses, and none of them cares about anyone's rate limit.

        Join the list

        1. Open your gotchi's settings page (http://tokibot-xxxx.local:8765/settings, or 192.168.77.1:8765 over USB).
        2. Tick Join the highscores and save. The list shows the name you gave it on the social page.
        3. That's it. The gotchi posts once an hour when it has Wi-Fi. A gotchi on the USB cable only gets posted by the client on your computer.

        Untick it and the gotchi asks the site to delete its entry.

        How the scores stay fair

        • Food points, not raw tokens. Input, output and cache writes count fully, cache reads a tenth. A long session that rereads its cache does not drown everyone else.
        • Fixed periods. Weeks start on Monday, months on the first (UTC). The lists never look at the 5-hour or weekly limits of any plan.
        • Estimates stay out of Glutton. Harnesses that do not report token counts (Copilot, Cursor, Devin, Kiro, Cline, Amp) feed the gotchi an estimate. It counts for XP and Gourmet, not for Glutton.
        • Signed by the gotchi. Every post is signed with the gotchi's own key, so nobody can post under its name. The site checks that the numbers are possible. It is a game, not an audit: we cannot prove what a gotchi ate.
        • Little data. Name, level, XP, best streak, and food and XP per day. No projects, no prompts, no code, and the list keeps no IP address.
